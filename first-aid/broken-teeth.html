Steps for Treating Broken Teeth

  1. Rinse the mouth with warm water to clean the area.
  2. Apply a cold compress to the outside of the mouth to reduce swelling.
  3. If a piece of tooth is broken, keep it in milk or saliva and take it to the dentist.
  4. Cover any sharp edges with dental wax or sugar-free gum.
  5. Visit a dentist as soon as possible to prevent infection and further damage.

💊 Recommended Medications:

  • Pain Relief: Ibuprofen, Acetaminophen
  • Antiseptic Mouthwash: Chlorhexidine rinse
  • Dental Wax: Temporary covering for sharp edges
