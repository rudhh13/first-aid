Steps for Treating Drowning

  1. Remove the person from the water as quickly as possible.
  2. Check for responsiveness and breathing.
  3. If the person is not breathing, start CPR immediately.
  4. Give rescue breaths: Pinch the nose shut, make a seal over the mouth, and give 2 slow breaths.
  5. Perform chest compressions: 30 compressions at a rate of 100-120 per minute.
  6. Continue CPR until the person starts breathing or emergency responders arrive.
  7. Call emergency services immediately.

💊 Recommended Actions:

  • Oxygen Therapy: If available, administer oxygen.
  • IV Fluids: Medical professionals may administer fluids to stabilize the person.
  • Antibiotics: In case of aspiration pneumonia, a doctor may prescribe antibiotics.
