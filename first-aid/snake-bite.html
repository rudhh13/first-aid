Steps for Treating a Snake Bite

  1. Keep the victim calm and still to slow the spread of venom.
  2. Call emergency services immediately.
  3. Keep the affected limb lower than the heart and immobilized.
  4. Loosely wrap a clean bandage around the bite area.
  5. Do not try to suck out the venom or apply ice.
  6. Avoid applying a tourniquet or cutting the wound.
  7. Transport the victim to the nearest hospital as soon as possible.

💊 Recommended Medications (Administer Only in a Medical Setting):

  • Antivenom: Specific to the type of snake, administered in a hospital.
  • Pain Relievers: Acetaminophen (Avoid NSAIDs like ibuprofen or aspirin).
  • IV Fluids: Used in severe cases to maintain blood pressure.
