Move the person to a warm, dry place as soon as possible.
  • Remove wet clothing and replace it with dry, warm blankets or clothing.
  • Provide warm, non-alcoholic, non-caffeinated beverages if the person is conscious.
  • Use heating pads or warm compresses on the chest, neck, and groin areas.
  • Seek emergency medical help immediately if symptoms worsen.

    • 💊 Recommended Medications:

    • Warm IV Fluids: Used in severe cases of hypothermia in a hospital setting.
    • Oxygen Therapy: Helps in cases where breathing is affected due to extreme cold.
    • External Warming Devices: Electric blankets or warming devices may be used under medical supervision.
