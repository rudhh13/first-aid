Steps for Treating Seizures

  1. Stay calm and ensure the person's safety.
  2. Move objects away to prevent injury.
  3. Place the person on their side to keep the airway clear.
  4. Do not restrain the person or put anything in their mouth.
  5. Time the seizure—if it lasts more than 5 minutes, call emergency services.
  6. Seek medical help if it’s the person's first seizure or if they have difficulty breathing after the episode.

💊 Recommended Medications:

  • Emergency Medication: Diazepam (rectal), Midazolam (buccal)
  • Long-Term Control: Levetiracetam, Valproate, Carbamazepine
  • Rescue Therapy: Clonazepam (if prescribed by a doctor)
