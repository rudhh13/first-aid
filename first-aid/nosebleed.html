Steps for Treating Nosebleeds

  1. Have the person sit upright and lean slightly forward.
  2. Pinch the soft part of the nose for 10-15 minutes.
  3. Apply a cold compress to the nose and cheeks.
  4. Avoid blowing the nose for several hours.
  5. Seek medical help if bleeding lasts more than 20 minutes.

💊 Recommended Medications:

  • Vasoconstrictor Nasal Sprays: (e.g., Oxymetazoline) to reduce bleeding.
  • Saline Nasal Spray: To keep nasal passages moist.
  • Petroleum Jelly: Apply inside the nostrils to prevent dryness.
