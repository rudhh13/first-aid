Steps for Assisting a Stroke Victim
  1. Recognize the symptoms using the FAST method:
    • Face: Ask the person to smile. Check for facial drooping.
    • Arms: Ask them to raise both arms. Check if one drifts downward.
    • Speech: Ask them to repeat a simple sentence. Look for slurred speech.
    • Time: If any signs appear, call emergency services immediately.
  2. Call emergency services immediately.
  3. Keep the person calm and lying on their side.
  4. Monitor their breathing and pulse.
  5. Do not give food, drink, or medication unless instructed by a doctor.

💊 Recommended Medications (Administer Only in a Medical Setting):

  • Blood Thinners: Aspirin (only if advised by a doctor).
  • Clot-Busting Drugs: Alteplase (tPA) - must be given in a hospital.
  • Blood Pressure Medication: Prescribed by medical professionals.
