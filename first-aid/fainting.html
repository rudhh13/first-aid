Lay the person on their back and elevate their legs to improve blood flow.
  • Loosen tight clothing and ensure they have fresh air.
  • If conscious, offer water and reassure them.
  • If unconscious for more than a minute, seek medical help.
  • Do not allow them to stand up too quickly.

    • 💊 Recommended Medications (If Needed):

    • Glucose Tablets: Useful if fainting is due to low blood sugar.
    • Iron Supplements: For anemia-related fainting.
    • Electrolyte Drinks: To restore hydration.
