Ensure your own safety before assisting.
  • Remove any contaminated clothing and rinse the affected area with running water for at least 20 minutes.
  • Flush the eyes with clean water if chemicals get into them.
  • Cover the burn with a sterile, non-stick dressing.
  • Avoid applying ointments or creams unless directed by a medical professional.
  • Seek emergency medical help if the burn is severe or widespread.

    • 💊 Recommended Medications:

    • Pain Relief: Acetaminophen, Ibuprofen
    • Topical Treatment: Silver sulfadiazine (if prescribed by a doctor)
    • Eye Wash: Sterile saline solution (for eye exposure)
