Steps for Treating Sprains & Strains

  1. Apply an ice pack wrapped in a cloth to the injured area for 15-20 minutes.
  2. Use a compression bandage to help reduce swelling.
  3. Elevate the injured limb above heart level to minimize swelling.
  4. Avoid putting weight on the affected area and rest as much as possible.
  5. Seek medical attention if severe pain, swelling, or difficulty moving persists.

💊 Recommended Medications:

  • Pain Relief: Ibuprofen, Acetaminophen
  • Anti-inflammatory Creams: Diclofenac gel, Menthol-based ointments
  • Muscle Relaxants: Cyclobenzaprine (if prescribed)
