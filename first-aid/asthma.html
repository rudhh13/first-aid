Help the person sit upright and stay calm.
  • Assist them in using their prescribed inhaler (preferably a reliever inhaler like albuterol).
  • Encourage slow, deep breaths to ease breathing difficulty.
  • Loosen any tight clothing around the chest and neck.
  • Seek emergency medical help if symptoms do not improve within 5-10 minutes or worsen.

    • 💊 Recommended Medications:

    • Bronchodilators: Albuterol (Ventolin), Levalbuterol
    • Steroids (for severe cases): Prednisone, Budesonide
    • Oxygen Therapy: Provided in medical emergencies
