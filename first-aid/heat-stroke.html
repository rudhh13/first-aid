Move the person to a cool, shaded area or indoors.
  • Have the person drink cool water or an electrolyte-rich drink if conscious.
  • Apply cool, wet cloths to the skin or use fans to lower body temperature.
  • If possible, immerse the person in a cool bath or spray them with water.
  • Seek emergency medical assistance immediately.

    • 💊 Recommended Medications:

    • Electrolyte Replacement: Oral rehydration salts (ORS) or sports drinks.
    • Cooling Agents: Ice packs applied to armpits, neck, and groin.
    • Medical Supervision: IV fluids and temperature regulation in severe cases.
