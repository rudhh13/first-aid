Steps for Treating Poisoning
  1. Identify the type of poison (medications, chemicals, food, gas, etc.).
  2. Call emergency services or a poison control center immediately.
  3. Do not induce vomiting unless instructed by a medical professional.
  4. If the poison was swallowed, provide water or milk if advised by a professional.
  5. If poisoning is due to inhalation (e.g., carbon monoxide), move the person to fresh air immediately.
  6. For skin exposure, rinse the affected area with running water for at least 15 minutes.

💊 Recommended Medications:

  • Activated Charcoal: Used in some cases of poisoning to absorb toxins (only if advised by a doctor).
  • Antidotes: Specific antidotes may be required for certain poisons (e.g., Naloxone for opioid overdose).
  • Oxygen Therapy: Used for inhalation poisoning such as carbon monoxide exposure.
