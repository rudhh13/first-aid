Wash your hands and the affected area with mild soap and water.
  • Cover the blister with a clean, non-stick bandage to prevent infection.
  • Avoid popping the blister, as it protects the underlying skin.
  • If the blister bursts, clean the area and apply antibiotic ointment.
  • Reduce friction by wearing comfortable, well-fitting shoes or using protective padding.

    • 💊 Recommended Medications:

    • Antibiotic Ointments: Bacitracin, Neosporin
    • Pain Relief: Ibuprofen, Acetaminophen
    • Skin Protectants: Petroleum jelly, Moleskin padding
