Steps for Treating Food Poisoning

  1. Drink plenty of fluids to prevent dehydration (water, electrolyte drinks, clear broth).
  2. Avoid solid foods until vomiting and diarrhea subside.
  3. Take oral rehydration salts (ORS) if necessary.
  4. Rest and allow your body to recover.
  5. Seek medical help if symptoms persist for more than 48 hours or worsen.

💊 Recommended Medications:

  • Anti-Diarrheal: Loperamide (Imodium)
  • Nausea Relief: Bismuth subsalicylate (Pepto-Bismol)
  • Rehydration: Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS), Pedialyte
  • Pain Relief: Acetaminophen for fever or aches
