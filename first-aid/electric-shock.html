Steps for Treating Electric Shock

  1. **Turn off the power source** before touching the person.
  2. **Do not touch the victim** with bare hands if they are still in contact with electricity.
  3. Call **emergency services** immediately.
  4. Check for **breathing and pulse**. If absent, begin **CPR**.
  5. Treat **burns** with cool water and cover with a sterile dressing.
  6. Keep the person **calm and lying down** while waiting for medical help.

Warnings:

  • Do **not** touch the victim until the power is off.
  • Do **not** use ice, butter, or ointments on burns.
  • Seek medical help even if the victim appears fine, as internal injuries may occur.
