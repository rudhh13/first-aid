Steps for Treating Puncture Wounds

  1. Wash your hands thoroughly before touching the wound.
  2. Rinse the wound gently with clean water.
  3. Apply gentle pressure with a sterile cloth to stop bleeding.
  4. Clean the area with antiseptic to prevent infection.
  5. Cover with a sterile bandage and monitor for signs of infection.
  6. Seek medical attention if the wound is deep, caused by a rusty object, or shows signs of infection.

💊 Recommended Medications:

  • Pain Relief: Acetaminophen, Ibuprofen
  • Antibiotic Ointment: Neosporin, Bacitracin
  • Tetanus Shot: Recommended if the wound is deep and the last shot was over 5 years ago
