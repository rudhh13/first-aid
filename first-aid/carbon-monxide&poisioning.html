Steps for Treating Carbon Monoxide Poisoning
  1. Immediately move the person to fresh air or a well-ventilated area.
  2. Call emergency services (911 or local emergency number) for medical assistance.
  3. Open windows and doors to increase ventilation and remove the source of carbon monoxide.
  4. If the person is unconscious but breathing, place them in the recovery position.
  5. If they are not breathing, begin CPR immediately until emergency help arrives.

💊 Recommended Medications:

  • Oxygen Therapy: Administered by medical professionals.
  • Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy: Used in severe cases to quickly remove CO from the blood.
  • Supportive Care: Fluids and medications for symptoms as directed by a doctor.
