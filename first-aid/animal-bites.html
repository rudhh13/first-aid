Wash the bite wound with soap and water for at least 5 minutes.
  • Apply an antiseptic solution to disinfect the wound.
  • Apply gentle pressure with a clean cloth if there is bleeding.
  • Cover the wound with a sterile bandage.
  • Monitor for signs of infection, such as redness, swelling, or pus.
  • Seek medical attention if the bite is deep, from a wild animal, or if rabies vaccination is needed.

    • 💊 Recommended Medications:

    • Antibiotics: Amoxicillin-Clavulanate for infection prevention
    • Pain Relief: Ibuprofen, Acetaminophen
    • Topical Treatments: Antiseptic ointment, Hydrogen peroxide
    • Vaccinations: Rabies vaccine, Tetanus shot if necessary
