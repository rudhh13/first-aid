Steps for Treating Eye Injuries

  1. Do not rub the eye, as this may cause more damage.
  2. Rinse the eye with clean, lukewarm water if a foreign object is present.
  3. Cover the eye with a sterile dressing or a clean cloth if an injury is severe.
  4. Avoid applying pressure to the injured eye.
  5. Seek immediate medical help if the injury is serious or vision is affected.

💊 Recommended Medications:

  • Lubricating Eye Drops: Artificial tears, Refresh Tears
  • Antibiotic Ointments: Erythromycin ophthalmic ointment
  • Pain Relief: Acetaminophen, Ibuprofen
