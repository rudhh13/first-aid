Steps for Treating Ear Injuries

  1. If there is bleeding, gently apply a clean cloth to stop it.
  2. Do not insert cotton swabs or objects into the ear canal.
  3. Apply a cold compress to reduce swelling.
  4. Use over-the-counter pain relievers if needed.
  5. Seek medical help if there is severe pain, hearing loss, or drainage from the ear.

💊 Recommended Medications:

  • Pain Relief: Ibuprofen, Acetaminophen
  • Antibiotic Ear Drops: If prescribed for infections
  • Anti-inflammatory Medications: To reduce swelling
