Steps for Treating Sunburn

  1. Move to a cool, shaded area immediately.
  2. Apply cool compresses or take a cool bath to soothe the skin.
  3. Use aloe vera gel or a moisturizing lotion to reduce dryness and pain.
  4. Drink plenty of water to stay hydrated and aid skin recovery.
  5. Take ibuprofen or acetaminophen to reduce pain and inflammation.
  6. Avoid further sun exposure until the burn heals.

💊 Recommended Medications:

  • Pain Relief: Ibuprofen, Acetaminophen
  • Topical Treatment: Aloe vera gel, Hydrocortisone cream
  • Moisturizers: Calamine lotion, Vitamin E cream
