Steps for Treating Insect Bites & Stings

  1. Move away from the area to avoid more bites or stings.
  2. Wash the affected area with soap and water.
  3. Apply a cold compress to reduce swelling and pain.
  4. Remove the stinger (if present) by scraping it off with a flat object.
  5. Take an antihistamine to reduce itching and allergic reactions.
  6. Seek medical help if there are signs of a severe allergic reaction (difficulty breathing, swelling of the face or throat).

💊 Recommended Medications:

  • Antihistamines: Diphenhydramine (Benadryl), Loratadine
  • Pain Relief: Ibuprofen, Acetaminophen
  • Topical Treatments: Hydrocortisone cream, Calamine lotion
  • Emergency Treatment: Epinephrine (EpiPen) for severe allergic reactions
