Steps for Treating Head Injuries & Concussions

  1. Keep the person still and calm.
  2. Apply a cold compress to reduce swelling.
  3. If there is bleeding, apply gentle pressure with a clean cloth.
  4. Watch for signs of a concussion: dizziness, confusion, or vomiting.
  5. Seek medical help if there is loss of consciousness or persistent symptoms.

💊 Recommended Medications:

  • Pain Relief: Acetaminophen (Avoid ibuprofen or aspirin due to bleeding risk)
  • Anti-inflammatory Medications: Only if recommended by a doctor
